Official Privacy Policy • Municipal Administrative & Civic Redressal Platform

Effective Date: November 11, 2026 | Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ChittorTech ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information in connection with the mobile application गढ़सेवा : Chittorgarh-Parishad (Package: com.chittortech.snp), developed for the administrative officials, ward councillors (Parshads), and citizens of Nagar Parishad Chittorgarh, Rajasthan.

1. Information We Collect

The application only collects data strictly necessary for civic governance, municipal issue tracking, and user account verification:

  • Administrative Credentials: Official email address, assigned municipal role (e.g., Chairman, Commissioner, Councillor, Executive Officer), and authentication tokens managed through secure Supabase infrastructure.
  • Citizen Grievance Information: Complainant name, mobile number, ward number, grievance description, problem category, and landmark/address solely for resolving civic complaints (e.g., sanitation, streetlights, water supply).
  • Verification & Audit Records: Two-step closure verification records including field inspection notes, citizen feedback ratings, and resolution timestamps.
  • Device & Network Information: Basic device parameters (OS version, network connectivity status) for error diagnostics and real-time database sync.

2. App Permissions Requested

The application requests only essential system permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to alert municipal officers and councillors about new complaints, ward escalations, and status changes in real-time.
  • Internet Access: Required to communicate securely via HTTPS/TLS with the encrypted Supabase database and sync ticket statuses.
  • Phone Call Intent (Optional / User-Initiated): Used only when an officer taps "Call Citizen" or "Call Ward Councillor" to launch the native device dialer with the phone number pre-filled. The app does not record or listen to calls.

3. How We Use the Information

All collected data is utilized exclusively for:

  • Routing citizen grievances to the appropriate municipal department and ward supervisor.
  • Monitoring Service Level Agreements (SLA) and turnaround times for municipal public services.
  • Notifying authorities and citizens of complaint resolution progress.
  • Maintaining an official, transparent municipal audit log.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share any personal information or grievance data with commercial third parties or advertisers.

Information is accessible only to:

  • Authorized municipal officials and field workers of Nagar Parishad Chittorgarh assigned to resolve the specific grievance.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure providers (Supabase / AWS) operating under strict encryption and privacy compliance standards.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • All network transmissions are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Authentication tokens are securely stored on-device using encrypted preferences.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures officials can only view and modify records pertinent to their administrative jurisdiction.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Grievance records are maintained as official municipal public records in compliance with applicable public record retention policies of Rajasthan. Users who wish to request data correction or deletion may contact our Data Protection Nodal Desk at contact@chittortech.in.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is designed for adult citizens, ward councillors, and municipal staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
